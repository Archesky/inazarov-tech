I'm Ilya, a DevOps engineer based in Moscow. For the past five-plus years
I've been working on infrastructure — Kubernetes clusters, CI/CD, databases
under real production load, and observability.

Along the way I've worked on high-load production environments — profiling
and optimizing PostgreSQL, ClickHouse, Cassandra, MongoDB and Redis,
automating infrastructure with Ansible and Terraform, running across multiple
clouds (AWS, Yandex Cloud, VK Cloud), setting up monitoring and alerting
(Prometheus/VictoriaMetrics, Grafana, OpenSearch), and tuning Kubernetes
resource usage (HPA/VPA/KEDA, Kyverno).

Education — National Research Nuclear University MEPhI, information systems
and programming.

I occasionally write short notes here about things I actually run into in
practice — no fluff, no pretense of being an exhaustive guide.
